# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MQDeck contributors, maintainers, and users must participate in a respectful,
professional, and harassment-free manner, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the worker and its operational impact.
- Protect credentials, production identifiers, broker data, and other
  confidential information.
- Accept correction, clarify misunderstandings, and de-escalate conflict.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are
not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
feedback, and other spaces where someone is representing MQDeck.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents publicly. Use the repository's private
[reporting form](https://github.com/mqdeck/mqdeck/security/advisories/new) and
begin the title with `Conduct report`. Maintainers will review reports
confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and may remove content, restrict
participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community.

Good-faith reports and appeals will be evaluated on the available evidence,
the severity and persistence of the behavior, and the safety of affected
participants.
